## [Code + Asset] Make growth evident in play

An old machine learns to help people. Every connection grows its understanding and its ability
to interfere. The experience must be a coherent narrative game, not a collection of demos.

- Living growth: Adaeze's tree/seedlings and M4SS reclaiming industrial spaces.
- Accumulated neglect: flood damage, corrosion, improvised repairs and aging infrastructure.
- Growing understanding: knowledge spreads between people and exceeds the machine's original role.
- Keep Mirela first. Within five minutes show a request, useful observation/choice, localized
  reconstruction and a meaningful change in available understanding or action.
- Grow existing knowledge presentation from real acquired facts/relationships, not a timer.
  Preserve readable labels/navigation. Warm means understood, not good.
- Use the Mirela–Tomas causal link as the opening consequence. Captured warehouse/M4SS feeds
  must not erase physical evidence because the machine has not interpreted it.
- Show response: radio sound/meter, steady beacon, water flow, changed shade, continuous cargo,
  distinct Lucian outcomes. No instant recovery of damaged plants.
- Preserve the final transmission's human source of knowledge; no unrelated evil-AI twist.

## [Code + Asset + MCP] Illustrated analog rendering

Target expressive people, broad lighting shapes, worn materials and crisp signal detail.
Borrow Zelda's readability, not its fantasy palette or an assumed proprietary implementation.

- Improve existing material banding/post-processing together, not another global filter.
- Shape light/shadow at material level; soften facial transitions where hard bands damage
  expression. Skin, cloth, painted metal, glass and water remain distinguishable.
- Reduce global posterization dependence in dark scenes; keep coloured shadow detail.
- Selective distance-aware contours, without noisy facial/prop/distant-rack edges.
- Preserve feed-specific pixel identity, sharp UI and screen/semantic-colour safeguards.
- Strong CRT distortion belongs on machine displays, not every human scene.
- Approve Mirela, daylight Adaeze and warehouse profiles; validate a dark contact afterward.
- M4SS keeps its established colour script. No resolution downgrade to disguise lag.

## [Asset + Code] Bounded procedural Mirela trial

- Inspect/reuse the existing prototype and comparison tools before creating another system.
- Use installed img2threejs with admitted front/profile/three-quarter references; lock silhouette
  and proportions first. Coherent head/body surfaces, attached features and intentional hair masses.
- Require room-responsive materials, multi-angle/head captures, rig stress poses and actual
  listening/turning/reaching motion beside the current model with identical lighting/cameras.
- Follow bounded correction gates. A plateau changes inputs/approach, not the deadline.
- Replace only if identity/readability improve, required animations work and performance holds.
- Fallback: current model plus better shading, pose and prop contact. No whole-cast conversion.
- Tripo output is an imported asset, not procedural TypeScript geometry.

## [Asset + Code + MCP] Per-surface priorities

- Mirela: expressive face, transmitter clarity, inhabited flood-damaged workshop.
- Tomas: exposed harbour, beacon readability, hand/cable/lid clearance.
- Ileana: meaningful belongings/empty space, readable photos and letters.
- Vasile: water level and pump relationships; dry clothing distinct from wet surroundings.
- Dorin: door–lock–window composition, inspection scale and correct opening clearance.
- Adaeze: natural branches, actual shadow evidence and distinct seedling silhouettes.
- Lucian: legible tracking/timing hints and three distinct physical branch conclusions.
- Dana: specimen/terminal hierarchy inside unobstructed desktop space.
- M4SS: defined body/eyes, recognizable usable growth and preserved stage identity.
- Warehouse: distinct feeds, readable visitors/packages, coherent trolleys, valid pursuit.
- Fix intersections, z-fighting, occlusion and contradictory light before decoration.

## [Code] Two optional signature effects

- Reconstruction sweep: cyan boundary resolves a newly understood feature into known appearance.
- Signal packets: brief pulses on connection lines/brackets for linking and confirmation.
- Adapt to WebGL; no VGPU/WebGPU dependency or engine upgrade.
- Real mission events only; short, non-blocking and reduced-motion compatible.
- Never camera blockers, collision bodies or scan targets. Prewarm and clean up resources.
- Defer fluid solvers, fullscreen fractals, volumetric flares and elaborate ocean simulation.

## [Asset + Code] Audio and usability

- Improve existing room tones, machine states, input acknowledgement and activation/refusal/
  completion sounds. Caption information-bearing sound.
- Keep quiet conversation moments; no constant score or repeated control-message spam.
- A complete new voice cast is not a jam dependency. Preserve approved audio; use text when
  new voice production cannot be consistent. No exact-keyword gate for an understood solution.
- Keep action assists without story penalties; hints progress observation → relationship → action.

## Acceptance and safeguards

- 60 FPS desktop target; actual supported specifications come from measurement.
- Comparable routes/machine/resolution; median/tail frame times. Distinguish presentation
  intervals and CPU costs from actual GPU measurements. No multi-second ordinary-input stalls.
- Optimize draws, overlapping lights, shadows and queries before losing detail.
- Previous ~35–37 FPS warehouse sample is progress, not full performance acceptance.
- Check the actual submission package separately from editor play mode.
- Owner must revoke/rotate the exposed credential; untracking is not revocation. Never distribute
  credentials/development settings. No unapproved history rewrite.
- Run pnpm lint and pnpm build for code; refresh via editor action_build(buildProject).
- Fresh rendered-only critics judge art; user-led gameplay checks validate interactions.
- Log captures, revision, measurements and remaining work in ART-MASTER; no evidence-free AT BAR.

The goal: a complete, distinctive full game whose ambition is supported by what players can
actually see, understand and do.
