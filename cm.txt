Hide the reaching cord

A pale blue line from the body to the tendril tip, drawn while the button
is held and the reach has not landed.

Worth naming why it stood out rather than just removing it: #8fd6e8 is
the only thing in this stage wearing a colour from outside the palette.
Everything else is the slime's greens, the lantern's lemon, or the room's
browns - so a hard cyan stroke over the top of all of it read as UI drawn
on the world rather than as part of the creature.

What goes with it is a feedback channel. It was the confirmation that a
hold had registered before the tendril landed. The grip flash and the
ring around a growth in range both still say that, which is why it can go
without a replacement - but if reaching starts to feel unresponsive, this
is the thing that was carrying it, and the answer is this ribbon in the
slime's own green rather than the return of the blue one.

cordGeometry is kept and unused, and says so. The cord was hidden for its
colour rather than its existence; deleting the builder would turn that
successor into a rewrite, and it took several passes to stop looking like
a wire.

The first version of this hiding cleared the geometry every frame, which
allocates a BufferGeometry per frame to say the same nothing. The node is
created empty in mount() and never filled, so the paint loop does not
touch it at all now.
