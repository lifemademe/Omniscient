# Reference — what each image is a bar FOR

A gauntlet critic is handed one of these and asked "what does the bar have that the artifact
does not". So each one has to say what it is evidence of, or a critic will judge the wrong
thing — most often the interface, because several of these are UI mockups and the interface is
not on the board.

**`ART-DIRECTION-v1.png` is the master.** It outranks every other image here and it outranks
ART-MASTER.md §1. World vision, character language, silhouette rule, three palettes, five
lighting moods, props, UI style, theme rules. When two references disagree, this one wins.

The two things it settles that nothing else does:

- **The period.** *A parallel Earth in the 1990s dreamed of the future — advanced, but analog,
  chunky, imperfect.* Not cyberpunk. Not 2077. Anything neon-futurist is out of bounds.
- **The duality.** Warm/messy/imperfect human world against a cold/vast/orderly AI realm, and
  **as knowledge grows the boundary fades.** That is an art rule wired to a mechanic, and it is
  the strongest idea in the set.
